## How I approach engineering

**Understand before rewriting.** Recover the actual business rules and dependencies before changing a legacy system.

**Design for change.** Favor explicit boundaries, maintainable abstractions, and solutions that teams can extend.

**Treat reliability as a feature.** Use appropriate integration patterns and pay attention to failure modes, data consistency, and operational behavior.

**Measure performance.** Investigate bottlenecks and validate improvements rather than relying on unverified performance claims.
